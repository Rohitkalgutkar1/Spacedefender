Privacy Policy

Space Defender · Effective date: October 25, 2026
In short: Space Defender does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Who we are

Space Defender is an arcade game created by Rohit Kalgutkar ("the developer"). If you have any question about this policy, contact: next.rohit1@gmail.com

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask you to create an account or enter a name, email address or any other personal detail, and it does not send any gameplay or personal data to the developer or to any third party.

What is stored on your device

  • Your high score, saved locally on your device so it is still there next time you play.
  • The game files, saved locally so the game can start quickly and work offline.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Hosting

The game files are delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when your device downloads or updates the game; this is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. The developer does not receive or use this information. After the game has loaded once, it can run offline.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
